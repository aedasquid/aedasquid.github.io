I AM NOT A ROBOT

2026 | Browser (PC & Touch-Enabled)

OVERVIEW

Made for Brackeys Game Jam 2026.2, I Am NOT A Robot is a deduction‑driven arcade game where players become Robert, a robot double agent exposing disguised robots among humans. Set in a dystopian noir future, players must observe NPC behaviors and use an EMP pistol to eliminate threats across five levels.

Robots lunge when approached too closely, giving only a brief chance to react, while harming humans turns them hostile. Players must balance quick reflexes with careful deduction to survive the hunt.

Released: August 2026 on Browser for PC. September 2026 for Browser Touch Support.

Download from: https://melonjoose.itch.io/iamnotarobot

Reception: Ranked #43 overall out of 2,244 entries with strong scores across all criterias. Players praised its polish, art style, and the deduction mechanic as a satisfying interpretation of the jam's theme "Trust No One".

Time spent on project: Total 4 weeks, 1 week for jam submission.

Team size: 4

Contributions: Gameplay design, programming, 3Cs, NPC behaviour, stage hazards, scene loader system, UI scripting, asset implementation, touch support implementation, and additional animations.
