SCHOOLBOUND

2025 | Windows and macOS

OVERVIEW

Made for a course assignment, Schoolbound is a narrative horror set in a Southeast Asian school, where the player is tasked to buy and complete a haunted collection of cards to break their curse of being bound to the school.

At night, the player must avoid the roaming spirits while exchanging items lost by students with the cryptic bookshop auntie for money. In the day, the player can buy card packs of the haunted collection from the same bookshop auntie, who becomes a warm caretaker for everyone.

Released: April 2025 on Windows and macOS

Download from: https://cobolo.itch.io/schoolbound

Reception: Featured in Lianhe Zaobao, Singapore’s largest Chinese-language newspaper. Over 4,000 downloads and 100,000+ streaming views within 3 months of launch.

Time spent on project: 12 weeks

Team size: 6

Contributions: Gameplay design, programming, events programming and scripting, gameloop and emergent horror design, enemy behaviours, player progression, player stealth mechanics, player interaction system, and sound design.
