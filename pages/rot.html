ROT

2023 (Browser)

OVERVIEW

Made for a course assignment, ROT is a minimalist endless arcade shooter. Players rotate to aim and fire, with their firing cooldown refreshed on successful hits to reward accuracy over spamming.

The variety of enemy shapes and behaviours are introduced over time as the player survives longer, such as strafing enemies, exploding chain-reactions, and shelled enemies.

Released: April 2023 on PC Browser.

Play on browser: https://aedanite.itch.io/rot

Reception: One of three games picked to be playable at an End of Semester show. Well-received and enjoyed by faculty and peers.

Time spent on project: 1 week

Solo project: Gameplay and programming.
