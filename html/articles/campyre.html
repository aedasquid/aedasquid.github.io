CAMPYRE

2024 (Browser)

OVERVIEW

Made for a course assignment, Campyre is an arcade-style horde survival, where the player fights off waves of enemies and bosses using whatever weapon they find.

A weapon freshly picked up from the ground allows the player to wield it to its full potential for a few seconds before its flame dwindles to a weaker version. The player can keep this rally up by constantly switching weapons, to keep up the heat.

Released: April 2024 on PC Browser.

Play on browser: https://aedanite.itch.io/campyre

Reception: Praised by faculty and peers for its polish, “juicy” animations, and snappy arcade gameplay.

Time spent on project: 2 weeks

Team size: 2

Contributions: Gameplay design, programming, game feel, and visual effects.
