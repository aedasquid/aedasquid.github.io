CAMPYRE

2024 (Browser)

OVERVIEW

Made for a course assignment, Campyre is an arcade-style horde survival, where the player fights off waves of enemies and bosses using whatever weapon they find.

A weapon freshly picked up from the ground allows the player to wield it to its full potential for a few seconds before its flame dwindles to a weaker version. The player can keep this rally up by constantly switching weapons, to keep up the heat.

Released: April 2024 on PC Browser.

Play on browser: https://aedanite.itch.io/campyre

Reception: Praised by faculty and peers for its polish, “juicy” animations, and snappy arcade gameplay.

Time spent on project: 2 weeks

Team size: 2

Contributions: Gameplay design, programming, game feel, and visual effects.

Asset credits: Under CC0 license or equivalent, sprites edited from Kevin's Mom's House, music from Tallbeard Studios, SFX from Shapeforms, Kronbits, and Leohpaz, and font by datagoblin.

ARCADE-STYLE

Meant for quick play in class with 3 weeks of development time.

  • Scoped to be played finish in a few minutes.
  • Focus on those few minutes with polish and game feel.

Themed around "fire" and "hot potato".

  • Gameplay should have the player keep moving and "stay warm".
  • Frequent enemy attacks to get player to dodge.
  • Temporary weapon buffs to encourage weapon swapping.

Emphasis on exaggerated motion and reactive combat.

  • Chaotic blocky fire visual effects that spread around.
  • Cartoon squash-and-stretch physics.

PLAYER GAMEPLAY

Four Weapons

  • Players start with a melee Torch.
  • Enemies can drop a Sword, Bow, or Staff for players to pick up.

Design Philosophy

  • Each weapon is meant to embody a distinct playstyle archetype.
  • Must be simple, intuitive, and satisfying to use.
  • Weapons are briefly empowered after being picked up.
    • Meant for players to feel strong and play aggressively.
    • Temporary buff so to encourage weapon swaps to stay empowered.
    • Depowered weapons must remain viable.

Balance Approach

  • Empowered weapons defeat common enemies in 1 to 2 hits.
  • Depowered weapons take about 3 hits, still capable of defeating enemies to get a weapon drop.

ENEMY GAMEPLAY

Design Philosophy

  • Enemies primarily horde the player, dealing contact or ability damage.
  • Must be satisfying punching bags.
  • Abilities must be fair and readable, with clear telegraphs and counterplay.

Readability

  • No enemy health bars. Instead, enemies appear more and more on fire as their health drops.
  • Subtle feedback as player's fire weapons deal damage.
